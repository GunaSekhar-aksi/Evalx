## 0. Read this first

- You are a **pre-final year student**. Your target is the **Summer 2027 internship** (May to July 2027). Most of these open **between July and December 2026**, so the window is **right now**.
- Most companies review applications **on a rolling basis**. Applying in week 1 beats applying in week 6, even if the deadline says "open."
- **Always apply on the official careers page.** Aggregator sites are only useful for finding roles.
- **Never pay anyone** for a referral, an "internship guarantee," or "application assistance."
- Links in Section 2 are **careers home pages**. Search there for `intern`, `2027`, or `2028`.

## 5. Rules that actually get you selected

1. **Apply within 7 days** of a posting going live.
2. **One-page resume.** Two real projects with numbers ("cut API latency 40%"), not "Todo App."
3. **DSA:** arrays, strings, hashing, two pointers, trees, graphs, DP. Most intern OAs are 2–3 problems in 60–90 minutes.
4. **Use `long` and handle the modulo** when a problem says "return modulo 10^9 + 7."
5. **Set job alerts** on every careers page above. Checking aggregator blogs after a role closes is how people miss them.
6. **Track everything** in a sheet: company, role, date applied, status, OA date.
